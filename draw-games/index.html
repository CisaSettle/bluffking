彩票与骰宝：公开过程，独立复核

draw-games/ 是现有 BluffKing 开源仓库的子目录。这里公开开奖输入、确定性抽样、中奖计算和购票封盘规则，并提供无需连接 BluffKing 的离线验证器。覆盖定期开奖、刮刮乐、骰宝、鱼虾蟹、百家乐、俄罗斯转盘、老虎机、水果机、娃娃机和 2048；金豆无现金价值，刮刮乐还可赢取站内会员时长。

定期开奖使用 bluffking-lottery-drand-v5-instant。开奖后可从「我的彩票」下载证明并与购票回执核对。线上组件版本与提交见发布清单。

代码采用根目录的 AGPL-3.0-only 许可证，品牌规则见 商标说明。仓库不含玩家数据、运行密钥、未揭示种子或未来实际开奖结果。

刮刮乐、单人骰宝先取得持久化的服务器种子承诺，再由浏览器安全随机生成 32 字节玩家种子。请求绑定承诺、玩家种子和购买／投注条件，浏览器在请求前保存回执。公布服务器种子后，可重算结果及兑奖。刮刮乐单张全部揭示后才公开完整种子；5／10 张批量购买直接揭示。服务器收到玩家种子后能够计算结果，承诺不能强制其继续服务或支付。
双色球及其他定期开奖游戏、期号（即开奖时间）、协议版本、停售时间、drand 链和未来轮次组成固定计划。服务器在接受彩票前固定该计划，购买回执与票据保存同一计划。验证指定轮次的 BLS 签名，按公开算法生成号码。没有可选择的服务器种子；只等待已选轮次，不因服务异常换轮次或改用系统随机数。即使运营方不提供结果，持有相应公共信标仍可自行重算。

定期开奖的固定输入

当前五种定期开奖不再按固定时刻开奖：无人在售时，一次购票即开启新一期，开奖时间为 3 秒后；期号即开奖时间（Unix 毫秒），标签为新加坡时间 YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS。开奖前 1 秒停售，倒计时内的其他购票进入同一期，最后 1 秒内的购票开启下一期；有效票据的购买时间落在 [开奖 − 3 秒, 开奖 − 1 秒) 内。计划协议为 bluffking-lottery-drand-v5-instant。每注价格：双色球、大乐透、快乐8、福彩3D 2 金豆，TOTO 1 金豆；奖金表直接按该价格计（含本金），倍数最高 99。计划选择在开奖时间或之后的第一轮 drand quicknet 信标（倒计时结束后 0–3 秒内公开），而不是请求时的“最新轮次”。

固定链标识为 52db9ba70e0cc0f6eaf7803dd07447a1f5477735fd3f661792ba94600c84e971，起始时间为 Unix 秒 1692803367，每轮 3 秒。beacon.rs 固定公钥并验证 bls-unchained-g1-rfc9380 签名；随机输入是 SHA-256(signature)，不信任 HTTP 响应自带的随机数字段。

lottery.rs 将计划承诺与信标随机输入绑定；proof.rs 定义带域区分、长度前缀的 SHA-256 输入和计数器流。范围抽样使用拒绝采样消除取模偏差，无放回抽样使用部分 Fisher–Yates。信标网络仍有门限参与者、时钟与可用性假设，详见 drand 安全模型。签名验证使用固定版本的 drand-verify 0.6.2。

后台在数据库事务与行锁之外进行有超时、响应大小上限和重试间隔的信标请求。无法取得或验证指定信标时，同一期继续待开奖；已公开计划与已完成结果受持久化约束保护，恢复与重试不另选结果。缺少预先公开计划的期次不进入开奖。

独立复核

俄罗斯转盘、老虎机、水果机、娃娃机和 2048 共用单人开奖协议。浏览器在提交前保存服务器种子承诺、自己的随机种子和本轮输入，结算后可导出并离线复算。2048 的证明核对初始随机种子。领取返还后，导出文件还可重放操作并核对按规则应得的返还。运营方仍可拒绝或中止请求。

本版本在游戏内「工具 → 彩票公平性验证」提供证明与开奖前回执的上传、粘贴和核对；彩票记录中的「在工具中验证」可直接带入证明。在线工具把输入提交服务器，调用与命令行相同的 文档及回执验证器。需要独立验证时，可下载开源代码 ZIP，解压后在仓库根目录执行以下命令（先安装 Rust/Cargo）；构建完成后的验证过程不联网。此说明描述当前源码功能，不代表生产部署状态。

从公开仓库根目录构建独立验证器。首次构建可能下载锁定依赖；运行验证器不联网，不需要数据库或 BluffKing 账号。

cargo build --locked --manifest-path draw-games/verifier/Cargo.toml
./draw-games/verifier/target/debug/draw-verify proof.json
./draw-games/verifier/target/debug/draw-verify proof.json --receipt pre-draw-receipt.json
  1. 在配套客户端保存承诺回执（刮刮乐与骰宝在投注前；五种号码彩票的计划承诺随购买回执返回，在开奖前保存；保存时间不早于选定信标轮次时间的回执，验证器拒绝作为开奖前证据）；开奖后下载证明。客户端可导出 {proof, receipt} 文件；也可分别提供证明与先前保存的回执。
  2. 验证器重算号码或骰子，并在证明包含投注／票据信息时复核成本与奖金。提供回执时还会比对原承诺、玩家种子及相应投注条件。
  3. 成功输出 outcome: "replayed"；完整核对回执时为 receipt_match: "matched"，只有开奖前承诺一致时为 "commitment_only"，承诺及下注或购票条款一致且缺少玩家种子或票号时为 "terms_only"，未提供时为 "not_provided"。不匹配或证明损坏会输出 "rejected" 并以退出码 1 结束。仅重算成功不代表拥有开奖前的证据。

定期开奖提供无需账号的 GET /api/draws/:game/:period/proof，返回 plan、proof 和 proof_status；状态为 pending、available 或 unavailable。这里不公开个人选票与账号信息。游戏历史中的个人票据可合入证明的 ticket 字段，用于核对中奖计算。

若运营方不提供开奖证明，从既定链的 https://api.drand.sh/52db9ba70e0cc0f6eaf7803dd07447a1f5477735fd3f661792ba94600c84e971/public/{round} 取得计划中的准确轮次，保存为 beacon.json，再运行以下命令。把 GAME 和 PERIOD 换成已保存计划中的游戏名和整数期号。命令检查轮次及签名，再输出可复核的完整开奖证明。

./draw-games/verifier/target/debug/draw-verify --derive-lottery GAME PERIOD beacon.json > proof.json
./draw-games/verifier/target/debug/draw-verify proof.json --receipt pre-draw-receipt.json

核对骰宝签名时，在开奖号码出现前保存名单及运营方 Ed25519 公钥（GET /api/history/draw-evidence-key），然后添加参数 --evidence-key TRUSTED_KEY_HEX。登录的下注人可凭轮次编号通过 GET /api/history/sicbo-round-proof?round_id=UUID 查询本人接单凭证及结算或作废证明。

证据保存与保证范围

浏览器回执只存于当前用户、当前浏览器，最多保留 256 条、30 天；清理浏览器数据或更换设备会影响可用性，需要长期保留时请下载文件。后台彩票票据与开奖证明按现有 30 天策略清理；骰宝房间内历史最多 60 轮，账本沿用服务原有保留策略。这里没有永久保存承诺，也没有全局只追加的公开证明日志。

本地回执不是签名时间戳。验证器能检查提供的文件是否符合算法及已保存输入，不能单独证明回执何时出现、谁拥有账号、钱是否实际记入钱包、服务一定完成开奖，或线上运行了哪一个二进制。缺少开奖前回执时，只能复核文件内部的一致性；定期开奖仍可检查固定轮次签名并独立推导结果。骰宝房间在封盘后等候锁定的 drand 信标。服务器能拒收请求或中止一轮，已受理的作废轮次有签名记录。鱼虾蟹等待原定信标轮次。百家乐证明只打开已发牌，服务器持有整靴。本人可从投注历史下载包含已结算投注的证明。

老虎机判奖和娃娃机抓取资格、结果及派彩在公开规则库中计算。娃娃机浏览器物理动画用于展示开奖结果。名单抽奖在浏览器运行。

Lottery and Sicbo: public process, independent replay

draw-games/ is a subdirectory of the existing BluffKing open-source repository. It publishes draw inputs, deterministic sampling, prize calculation and purchase cutoff rules, together with an offline verifier that needs no connection to BluffKing. It covers number lotteries, scratch cards, Sic Bo, Fish Prawn Crab, Baccarat, Roulette, Slots, Fruit Machine, Claw and 2048. Beans have no cash value; scratch cards can also award in-app membership time.

Scheduled draws use bluffking-lottery-drand-v5-instant. Download the proof after the draw from My tickets and compare it with your purchase receipt. See the live release manifest for component versions and commits.

Code uses the root AGPL-3.0-only license; see the trademark policy. The repository contains no player data, runtime keys, unrevealed seeds or actual future draw results.

Fixed scheduled-draw inputs

The five scheduled games no longer draw on a wall-clock schedule. A purchase while nothing is on sale opens a period whose draw is 3 seconds later; the period id is the draw time in Unix milliseconds, labelled YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS in Singapore time. Sales close 1 second before the draw: other purchases during the countdown join the same period, and one in the last second opens the next. A valid ticket was bought within [draw − 3 s, draw − 1 s). The plan protocol is bluffking-lottery-drand-v5-instant. A stake costs 2 beans (SSQ, Daletou, Happy8, Fucai3D) or 1 bean (TOTO); prize tables are stated at that price, stake included, and the multiplier is at most 99. The plan selects the first drand quicknet round scheduled at or after draw time (published 0–3 seconds after the countdown ends), never the relay's “latest” round.

The pinned chain is 52db9ba70e0cc0f6eaf7803dd07447a1f5477735fd3f661792ba94600c84e971, with genesis Unix second 1692803367 and a three-second period. beacon.rs pins its public key and verifies the bls-unchained-g1-rfc9380 signature. Randomness is SHA-256(signature); the relay's supplied randomness field is not trusted.

lottery.rs binds the plan commitment to beacon randomness. proof.rs specifies domain-separated, length-prefixed SHA-256 inputs and a counter stream. Rejection sampling removes modulo bias; partial Fisher–Yates samples without replacement. Beacon unpredictability and availability still depend on threshold participants and timing assumptions: see the drand security model. Signature verification uses pinned drand-verify 0.6.2.

Beacon requests have timeouts, response-size limits and retry spacing, and run outside database transactions and row locks. If the selected beacon cannot be obtained or verified, the same draw remains pending. Persistence constraints protect published plans and completed results; retries and recovery do not choose another result. Periods without a previously published plan do not enter drawing.

Independent verification

This version provides proof and pre-draw receipt import under Tools → Lottery fairness verification. “Verify in Tools” on a draw record carries its proof directly into the tool. Online verification sends the input to the server and calls the same document and receipt verifier as the CLI. For independent verification, download the source ZIP, extract it, and run the following commands from the repository root with Rust/Cargo installed. Verification after building is network-free. This describes the source in this version, not its production deployment status.

Build the independent verifier from the public repository root. An initial build may download locked dependencies; running the verifier is network-free and needs neither a database nor a BluffKing account.

Roulette, Slots, Fruit Machine, Claw and 2048 share the instant-draw protocol. The app saves the server commitment, browser seed and play inputs before submission. Export the proof after settlement to replay it offline. For 2048 the proof covers the initial random seed and, after the player claims a refund, replays moves to verify the target and expected refund. The operator can refuse or abort a request.

cargo build --locked --manifest-path draw-games/verifier/Cargo.toml
./draw-games/verifier/target/debug/draw-verify proof.json
./draw-games/verifier/target/debug/draw-verify proof.json --receipt pre-draw-receipt.json
  1. Save the commitment receipt in the matching client (before the bet for scratch cards and Sicbo; for the five number lotteries the plan commitment comes back with the purchase receipt and is saved before the draw; the verifier rejects a receipt saved at or after the selected beacon round's time as pre-draw evidence), then download the proof after the draw. The client can export a {proof, receipt} bundle; separate proof and previously saved receipt files also work.
  2. The verifier reconstructs numbers or dice and, when wager/ticket details are present, recomputes costs and prizes. A supplied receipt is compared with the original commitment, player seed and applicable wager terms.
  3. Success reports outcome: "replayed". receipt_match is "matched" for a full receipt match, "commitment_only" when only the commitment can be compared, "terms_only" when the commitment and terms match but the player seed or ticket ID is missing, and "not_provided" when no receipt was supplied. A mismatch or invalid proof reports "rejected" and exits with code 1. Successful replay alone does not establish possession of pre-draw evidence.

Scheduled draws expose account-free GET /api/draws/:game/:period/proof, returning plan, proof and proof_status: pending, available or unavailable. This endpoint exposes no private selections or account data. A personal history ticket can be attached as the proof's ticket field to check its prize calculation.

If the operator withholds its proof, obtain the exact round from the saved plan at https://api.drand.sh/52db9ba70e0cc0f6eaf7803dd07447a1f5477735fd3f661792ba94600c84e971/public/{round} and save it as beacon.json. Replace GAME and PERIOD below with the saved game's name and integer period. The command verifies the round and signature, then emits a complete draw proof.

./draw-games/verifier/target/debug/draw-verify --derive-lottery GAME PERIOD beacon.json > proof.json
./draw-games/verifier/target/debug/draw-verify proof.json --receipt pre-draw-receipt.json

Pin the published Sicbo Ed25519 public key (GET /api/history/draw-evidence-key) before the dice are known and verify with --evidence-key TRUSTED_KEY_HEX. Participants can fetch their own signed acceptance and the settled or voided round evidence from GET /api/history/sicbo-round-proof?round_id=UUID.

Evidence retention and guarantees

Browser receipts belong to the current user in that browser, with limits of 256 entries and 30 days. Clearing browser storage or changing devices affects availability; download files for longer retention. Server lottery tickets and draw proofs follow the existing 30-day cleanup policy. Sicbo room history retains up to 60 rounds; ledger records follow the service's existing retention policy. This is neither permanent evidence storage nor a global append-only public proof log.

Verification checks the supplied data against the algorithm and saved inputs. A signed Sicbo receipt attests to the operator's own acceptance time, and a separately saved pre-pulse roster supplies evidence of when you received it. The verifier checks a fixed post-lock Quicknet pulse. The operator may still refuse wagers or abort service. Accepted voided rounds have signed records. A replay alone cannot prove account ownership, wallet credit or the deployed binary. Fish Prawn Crab waits for its fixed beacon round. Baccarat proofs open only dealt cards while the server holds the shoe. Personal betting-history proofs also include the settled wager.

The public library calculates slot prizes and claw eligibility, catches and payouts. The browser animates the claw result. The separate name picker runs in the browser.
